# Selected Publications

A selection of publications and research outputs related to my work on evolved stellar populations, galaxy evolution, and circumstellar dust. For the complete and up-to-date list, please see [NASA ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/) and my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Hamidreza+Mahani).

## Circumstellar dust and evolved stars

- **Mahani et al. (2025), “Long-period variable stars in NGC 147 and NGC 185-II. Their dust production.”**. *The Astrophysical Journal*. [arXiv:2508.05596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05596) · [DOI: 10.3847/1538-4357/adfa2b](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adfa2b).

- **Rostami et al., “SEDust: A DUSTY-Based SED-Fitting Pipeline for Dust and Mass Loss from AGB and LPV Stars.”** *The Astrophysical Journal*, accepted. The pipeline and associated research are available through the [SEDust GitHub repository](https://github.com/hmahani/SEDust).

- **Javadi et al. (2023), “The Isaac Newton Telescope Monitoring Survey of Local Group Dwarf Galaxies. VI. The Star Formation History and Dust Production in Andromeda IX.”** [The Astrophysical Journal](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4357/acbbc9).

## Initial mass function and stellar systems

- **Mahani et al. (2021), “Do ultracompact dwarf galaxies form monolithically or as merged star cluster complexes?”** [Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article-abstract/502/4/5185/6132256).

- **Hasani Zonoozi, Mahani & Kroupa (2019), “Was the Milky Way a chain galaxy? Using the IGIMF theory to constrain the thin-disc star formation history and mass.”** [Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/483/1/46/5144227).
