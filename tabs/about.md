 I am a postdoctoral researcher in observational astronomy at IPM, passionate about studying galaxy formation and evolution using stellar population synthesis techniques. I have a genuine eagerness to meet new people, learn from their perspectives, and continuously expand my knowledge. 


## Current position

I am a Senior Postdoctoral Researcher in observational astronomy at the School of Astronomy, Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM), Tehran. My research focuses on evolved stars, circumstellar dust, stellar mass loss, and resolved stellar populations in nearby galaxies, combining multiwavelength observations with computational modelling and statistical methods.

## Research background

My research experience spans stellar population modelling, the initial mass function, galaxy evolution, and the mass loss and dust production of evolved stars. During my doctoral research, I investigated the evolution of starburst galaxies in the integrated galactic initial mass function (IGIMF) framework. My subsequent work has increasingly focused on connecting resolved stellar populations and time-domain observations to the dust lifecycle of galaxies.

## Methods and expertise

- Multiwavelength photometry and spectral energy distribution (SED) analysis
- AGB stars, long-period variables, circumstellar dust, and stellar mass loss
- Radiative-transfer modelling with DUSTY-based workflows
- Bayesian parameter estimation and Markov chain Monte Carlo methods
- Stellar population synthesis, statistical analysis, and scientific Python
- Optical time-domain observations and astronomical instrumentation
