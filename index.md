## Research and scientific interests

I am a **Senior Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Astronomy, Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM), Tehran**. My research connects the evolution of resolved stellar populations with the lifecycle of circumstellar dust and the chemical evolution of galaxies.

My work focuses on **asymptotic giant branch (AGB) stars, long-period variables (LPVs), stellar mass loss, dust production, and the initial mass function (IMF)**. I combine multiwavelength observations, time-domain photometry, radiative-transfer modelling, and statistical inference to investigate how evolved stars return material to their host galaxies.

### Research highlights

- **Circumstellar dust and mass loss:** developing methods to infer luminosities, optical depths, and mass-loss rates from the spectral energy distributions of evolved stars.
- **Resolved stellar populations:** using variable stars and multiwavelength catalogues to study AGB populations and dust production in Local Group galaxies.
- **Research software:** developing [SEDust](https://github.com/hmahani/SEDust) and the Bayesian extension MCM-SEDust for reproducible analysis of dusty evolved stars.
- **Observational astronomy:** contributing to optical monitoring and instrumentation efforts, including observations with the INO340 telescope.
